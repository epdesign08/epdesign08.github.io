Privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This page covers the EBEN Labs website. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked below, which explains what that app does with your information.

The short version

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads, and it doesn't ask you for any information. We don't track you.

What happens when you visit

  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser details when your browser loads the page.
  • Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads them.
  • We don't see or store any of this ourselves.

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply and help you. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your email with anyone.
